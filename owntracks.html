OwnTracks Setup

Lets your friends follow you while your iPhone is locked and playing music. Do this once, at home, the night before or the morning of the race.

This page needs the tracking code. Open it from the “Set up OwnTracks” button on your runner page.

1

Install OwnTracks

It's free, made by the open-source OwnTracks project.

Open in App Store
2

Load the race settings

Tap the button, then tap Open when your phone asks to open OwnTracks.

Load settings into OwnTracks

Then come back to this page (top-left “◀ Safari” on iPhone).

3

Allow location “Always”

  • iPhone Settings › OwnTracks › Location: choose Always and turn on Precise Location.
  • Turn Low Power Mode off during the race; it slows background updates.
4

Test it

In OwnTracks, tap the send / publish button (the arrow icon at the top of the map) to send your location once. This page shows when it arrives.

Waiting for a location from OwnTracks…
5

On race day

  • Leave OwnTracks on Quiet until you're at the start line. That way the drive there doesn't use up updates.
  • On your runner page, tick OwnTracks is set up on this phone.
  • Tap Start race at the gun. Your phone asks to open OwnTracks: tap Open. That switches it to Move mode (live tracking).
  • Lock the phone and enjoy your music.
  • At the finish, unlock, open the runner page and tap Finish race twice. It switches OwnTracks back to Quiet.

The mode button is at the top left of the OwnTracks map: it cycles Quiet, Manual, Significant and Move. Choose Move at the start line.
